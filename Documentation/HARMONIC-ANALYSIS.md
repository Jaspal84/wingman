# Harmonic analysis increment

Implemented local DSP, not trained inference: bounded 120-second mono analysis, area-average sample-rate conversion to 22.05 kHz, 8192-point Hann STFT, log-interpolated spectral peaks, weighted tuning histogram, tuning-adjusted pitch-class features, eight chord templates with bass-root prior, three-frame label smoothing, temporal boundaries, and duration-weighted Krumhansl-Schmuckler major/minor key-profile correlation.

Choose a lane in the transport selector and click Detect chords/key. Analysis runs in a cancellable C++ worker; shutdown joins that worker. Detected labels appear above the waveform at their measured times. The editable progression quantizes region durations to quarter-beat increments at the current BPM, omits unreliable regions shorter than 200 ms, and supports up to 128 tokens. Silence/gaps are not encoded as rests in that progression; do not expect sample-aligned MIDI transcription across gaps. Live detected-region overlays retain their actual seconds. Reanalysis replaces the progression; cancel before starting another task.

Chord syntax: `C`, `Am`, `G7`, `Cmaj7`, `Dm7`, `Csus2`, `Csus4`, `Bdim`. Optional `^1` selects first inversion (`^0` through `^3` where valid); `:2` sets two beats. Example: `Cmaj7^1:2 Dm7:2 G7:4`. Default remains four beats, preserving old saved progressions. Click a chord box to edit root, quality, inversion and duration. Zoom and horizontal scroll change the timeline view. MIDI voicings and end-of-track duration follow these values.

Validation in Linux Debug: held root-position, equal-amplitude sine chords at all 12 roots and all eight qualities, detuned +15 cents: **96/96 longest-region labels correct**, tuning error under 6 cents. Synthetic tonic/subdominant/dominant cadences in all roots and both modes: **24/24 keys correct**. Silence rejection and cancellation are tested. A WAV-to-background-analysis integration test verifies that actual decoded chord audio updates the progression.

These are small synthetic reference sets, not estimates of commercial-song accuracy. No annotated licensed recording corpus has been supplied. Suspended chords share pitch sets under different roots; the bass prior resolves root-position test cases but inversions can be ambiguous. Percussive/dense audio, missing fundamentals, detuned instruments, modal harmony and key changes remain difficult. Scores are template/profile margins, not calibrated probabilities. The area-average downsampler is not a mastering-quality anti-aliasing filter. No genre-specific accuracy, beat tracking or recorded-audio benchmark is claimed. Analysis results are ephemeral; the corrected progression persists in version-1-compatible plugin state.
